Adaptive Metabolic Guardrails & Safe Deficit Protocol

Most calorie apps treat the human body like an inert furnace — prescribing crash 1,000 kcal deficits that shut down thyroid hormone (T3/T4), surge cortisol, cannibalize muscle tissue, and cause aggressive weight rebound.

Inspired by clinical metabolic wards, OpenCal AI enforces non-negotiable biological guardrails. Prior Labs TabPFN continuously evaluates endocrine strain and dynamically adjusts calorie targets to keep your metabolism functioning at peak capacity.

🔬
TabPFN Biological Sentinel: Continuously monitors rolling 7-day scale delta, non-exercise thermogenesis (NEAT), and protein adherence.
🛑
Anti-Starvation Floor

Strict floor of 1,500 kcal/day (men) and 1,200 kcal/day (women). The active deficit is mathematically clamped at a maximum 25% of your TabPFN dynamic TDEE to prevent metabolic shutdown.

🔄
Leptin Refeed Triggers

When weekly weight loss exceeds 1.0% body weight or rolling NEAT drops by >15%, the system schedules a 48-hour isocaloric maintenance refeed to restore leptin and glycogen.

🥩
Muscle Sparing Floor

Enforces a strict minimum of 1.8g to 2.2g of protein per kg of lean mass during deficit phases, eliminating muscle catabolism and preserving resting metabolic rate.

⚡
Electrolyte & Fiber Check

Grounds potassium, magnesium, and dietary fiber ratios to safeguard cardiac rhythm, neuromuscular transmission, and gut microbiome diversity during caloric restriction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about OpenCal AI

Learn how OpenCal AI combines computer vision, tabular foundation models, and clinical safety guardrails.

How does OpenCal AI eliminate calorie hallucinations compared to commercial apps?
Commercial calorie apps pass meal photos to a generic LLM with a prompt like "how many calories are in this?". The LLM guesses based on average internet text, leading to errors as high as 40%. OpenCal AI strictly decouples visual recognition from calculation: Google Gemma 2 identifies ingredients and estimates portion volume in grams. Those exact gram weights are then deterministically multiplied against verified USDA FoodData Central records using unique FDC identifiers.
What is Prior Labs TabPFN, and how does it calculate my true dynamic TDEE?
Traditional apps use static formulas (like Mifflin-St Jeor or Harris-Benedict) that multiply your weight by a generic activity multiplier (e.g. 1.2 or 1.55). In reality, human metabolism adapts dynamically: NEAT decreases, thyroid hormone shifts, and expenditure changes weekly. Prior Labs' TabPFN is a foundation tabular transformer trained on millions of synthetic datasets. By evaluating your rolling 30-day weight change and actual caloric intake, TabPFN performs in-context learning in 12ms to infer your real dynamic TDEE without any fine-tuning or retraining.
Why was OpenCal AI built for Hacktoberfest 2026 "Build for a Friend"?
Our friend Dave was frustrated with commercial fitness apps charging $35/month, nagging him with up-sells, hallucinating wildly on home-cooked meals, and selling his health telemetry to third parties. We built OpenCal AI as a fully open-source, private alternative that provides superior metabolic forecasting using state-of-the-art open models (Gemma 2, TabPFN) and verified USDA datasets.
How does OpenCal AI guarantee biological data privacy?
OpenCal AI is designed to run 100% locally on your own machine via Docker or standard Python environment. Your meal photos, scale weights, and metabolic logs remain stored in your sovereign local database. No data is ever sent to third-party ad networks, data brokers, or insurance companies.
How does the ElevenLabs Neural Voice Coach debrief work?
Rather than reading dry text notifications, OpenCal AI synthesizes an intelligent, motivational daily audio debrief. Powered by ElevenLabs' neural text-to-speech API, the coach reviews your daily protein adherence, metabolic adaptation rate, and projected days to goal, delivering actionable advice directly to your ears. If offline, it smoothly falls back to the browser's native Web Speech API.
Can I self-host OpenCal AI using Docker?
Yes! The entire project includes a production-ready Dockerfile. Simply clone the repository, run docker build -t opencal-ai . and docker run -p 8000:8000 opencal-ai. The FastAPI backend, static assets, USDA sample database, and TabPFN engine will launch locally.
